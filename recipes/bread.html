Milk bread

Description

Simple milk bread recipe

Ingredients

  • Yeast
  • Milk
  • Bread Flour
  • All purpose flour
  • Water
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Egg
  • Have a bread maker

Steps

  1. Get bread maker container
  2. Put water
  3. Put milk
  4. Put egg
  5. Put bread flour
  6. Put all purpose flour
  7. Put sugar
  8. Put salt
  9. Put yeast
  10. Set bread maker to soft
