Udon Noodle Recipe

Description

Simple udon noodle soup recipe

Ingredients

  • Udon Noodle
  • Water
  • Dashi Powder
  • Pot
  • Green Onion
  • Chicken Stock
  • garlic

Steps

  1. Boil water in pot
  2. Put dashi and chicke stock and let simmer
  3. Cut green onion and garlic while waiting
  4. Put udon and let it boil for 2 min
  5. Put green onion and sliced pork
  6. Enjoy
